Meet the founder

Doc & Writer was founded in 2020 by Alexander Dade, PMP, who leads our client work.

AD

Alexander Dade, PMP

Founder

Alexander has spent nearly a decade running knowledge management, support content, and program delivery. At Meta he ran eight portfolios in the global support operation, serving 14,000+ agents across 15+ markets, and led the knowledge work that took an internal AI agent from experiment to production.

Before that he migrated knowledge bases and product content at O-I Glass and Hunter Douglas, wrote API guides and runbooks at LexisNexis, and led technical writing at Concentrix.

He holds the PMP and a BA in Professional, Technical, Business and Scientific Writing from the University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee.

How we work

  • We measure before we write. Each engagement starts with a test set and a baseline, and each month ends with a re-test.
  • We fix in order of cost, so the first month already pays down the most expensive problems.
  • We leave owners behind. Each article has a named owner and a review date, so the content holds up after we finish.

A husband-and-wife firm

Alexander Dade founded Doc & Writer in 2020 and leads every engagement. Lydia Dade runs client relationships. You get one person for the work and one for the relationship.

AD

Alexander Dade, PMP

Founder

Alexander has spent eight years running knowledge management, support content, and program delivery. At Meta he ran eight portfolios in the global support operation, serving 14,000+ agents across 15+ markets, and built the knowledge foundation that took an internal AI agent from experiment to production.

Before that he migrated knowledge bases and product content at O-I Glass and Hunter Douglas, wrote API guides and runbooks at LexisNexis, and led technical writing at Concentrix, a contact center company.

He holds the PMP and a BA in Professional, Technical, Business and Scientific Writing from the University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee.

How we work

  • Alexander does the work himself. On larger programs he may add US-based writers, and he reviews their work before it reaches you.
  • We measure before we write. Every program starts with a test set and a baseline, and every month ends with a re-test.
  • We leave an owner behind. Each article has a named owner and a review date, so the content holds up after we finish.

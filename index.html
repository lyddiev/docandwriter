How we check your answers

You get a spreadsheet you can verify line by line, with the source for every verdict.

  1. Build the question set

    For the free check, we write 25 questions a customer would ask, based on your pricing page, changelog, and help center. The audit uses 100 questions from your own tickets.

  2. Ask your agent and search your help center

    We record exactly what each one says. If your agent sits behind a login, the free check covers the help center only.

  3. Grade each answer against your own sources

    An answer is wrong when it contradicts your pricing page, your release notes, or another article. When your sources disagree, your product expert decides.

  4. Trace each wrong answer to its article

    Outdated, duplicated, contradicted, or missing. That tells us which articles to fix first.

FAQ

Anything else, ask us on the call.

What do you need for the free answer check?

Your help center address, and where customers reach your AI agent if it is public. No logins, no data exports, and no access to your systems.

How do you decide an answer is wrong?

We grade each answer against your own published sources: your pricing page, release notes, and the rest of your help center. When your sources disagree with each other, we flag it and your product expert makes the call.

Does it matter which AI agent or help center platform we use?

No. We test the agent the way your customers use it and fix the content it reads from. Our team has worked in Salesforce Knowledge, Confluence, SharePoint, Adobe Experience Manager Guides, Heretto, and Git with Markdown.

Do you publish the changes, or do we?

Either. With editor access we publish directly, or we hand you changes ready to paste. Nothing goes live without your approval.

What if the audit is not useful?

Tell us, and we refund the fee.

Can we skip the audit and start the monthly plan?

Yes. Your first month then starts with the audit.

Who will we work with?

One senior knowledge lead runs your account from the first call through every monthly review, and all work is reviewed before it reaches you.

What does the contract look like?

A short services agreement and a one-page scope. We sign your NDA or provide ours. The monthly plan has a three-month minimum, then runs month to month.

Do you work with agencies and implementation partners?

Yes, as a subcontractor or through referrals. See our partner page.

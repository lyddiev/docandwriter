What wrong answers cost you

Some AI support agents count a conversation as resolved when the customer leaves without asking for more help. Intercom’s Fin works this way. A customer who believes a wrong answer and leaves is counted as a success. The ones who do not believe it open a ticket, and your team answers the question again.

How it works

Three steps. You can stop after any of them.

  1. Free wrong-answer report

    Free. Five business days.

    We ask your public help center and AI agent 25 questions your customers ask, mark each answer right or wrong, and name the article behind every wrong one.

    You get a short report and a screen recording. No call required.

  2. Knowledge Diagnostic

    $7,500, fixed. Two weeks.

    We build a test set of 200 questions from your real tickets, score your content against it, and price the wrong answers with your own cost per contact.

    You get the baseline, an owner for every article, and a 90-day plan. The fee counts toward the program if you start within 30 days.

  3. 90-Day Answer Accuracy Program

    $8,500 a month for three months.

    We fix the articles that cost you the most first, keep content in step with every release, and re-test each month against the same 200 questions.

    After 90 days, Knowledge Ops continues month to month from $6,000.

What the program includes

Alexander leads every program. Lydia runs the relationship: scope, monthly reviews, and what we fix next.

  • A test set built from your tickets, scored at the start and again every month.
  • Fixes in order of cost, starting with the articles behind the most expensive wrong answers.
  • An owner and a review date for every article, so content stays current after the program ends.
  • Help center updates written and approved before each release ships.
  • Articles split and titled so your AI agent retrieves the right one.
  • A gap loop: when a support agent flags a missing or wrong article, it goes to an owner and is tracked to closure.
  • A monthly scorecard in your numbers: accuracy on the test set, tickets caused by wrong answers, and what those tickets cost.

Included at no extra charge

  • The 16-tag feedback triage system Alexander built for Meta’s support content
  • A launch checklist for putting an AI agent in front of customers
  • Onboarding and implementation guide templates
  • A re-test 30 days after the program ends

The accuracy guarantee

At the end of the Diagnostic we agree, in writing, on a target score for your test set. If the program has not reached it by day 90, we keep working at no charge until it does.

Two conditions: you publish approved changes within five business days, and you do not switch AI agent vendors during the program.

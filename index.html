Symptoms we fix

Each one has a probable cause and a remedy we build into Knowledge Ops.

Your AI agent gives confident wrong answers.

Probable cause

It answers from articles written before your last few releases.

Remedy

A monthly accuracy run, with fixes ranked by what each wrong answer costs.

Agents give five answers to the same question.

Probable cause

Duplicate articles, and no one owns any of them.

Remedy

One article per question, each with a named owner and a review date.

Help articles ship after the feature does.

Probable cause

Documentation sits outside the release process.

Remedy

A release trigger in Jira that opens a documentation task the day the release ticket does.

No one can say whether the help center works.

Probable cause

Page views are the only measure.

Remedy

A monthly report on accuracy, open gaps, and the tickets wrong answers caused.

Your knowledge base is moving to a new platform.

Probable cause

Migrations copy outdated content along with the good.

Remedy

An audit before the move, then a verified cutover. Our team has run migrations with zero downtime.

How it starts

Three steps, and no commitment until the second.

  1. Free assessment

    Send us your help center address. Within five business days you see 25 real customer questions, which ones your content answers wrong, and why.

  2. Month one: the audit

    We build a 200-question test set from your tickets, score your content, price the wrong answers, and assign an owner to each article.

  3. Every month after

    We fix what costs you the most, cover each release, re-test, and report.

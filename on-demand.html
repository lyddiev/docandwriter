Knowledge Ops

A monthly service that runs your knowledge base: testing, fixes, release content, ownership, and reporting.

What we do each month

  • Run the accuracy test against your help center and AI agent, and review each failure.
  • Fix the articles behind the most expensive wrong answers first.
  • Write and publish help content for each release before it ships.
  • Keep an owner and a review date on each article, and follow up on overdue ones.
  • Structure articles so your AI agent retrieves the right one.
  • Route gaps your support team flags to the right owner, and close them.
  • Send a one-page report in your numbers, followed by a review call.

Month one: the audit

We pull a sample of recent tickets, turn them into a 200-question test set, and score your help center and AI agent against it. You approve the questions and the scoring rules. Then we price the wrong answers with your cost per ticket, assign an owner to each article, and agree on a target score for day 90.

Automations we set up

  • Reminders to article owners at each review date, with overdue articles listed in your report.
  • A documentation task opened automatically from each release ticket in Jira.
  • Agent feedback routed to the article owner with a due date.
  • A monthly accuracy run, with a person reviewing each failure.

Our guarantee

If accuracy has not reached the target we agree on by day 90, we keep working at no charge until it does. It applies when approved changes are published within five business days and your AI agent vendor stays the same during the first 90 days.

What we need from you

  • Editor access to your help center or knowledge base
  • An export of recent support tickets
  • A subject matter expert for about two hours a week to approve changes

Platforms

Salesforce Knowledge and Agentforce, Confluence, SharePoint, Adobe Experience Manager Guides, Heretto, DITA XML, and Git with Markdown. The test and the content work apply to any help center or AI agent.

Questions

Can we start with a one-time project?

Yes. We take on help center rebuilds, onboarding and implementation guides, and content migrations as fixed-price projects, quoted after a call.

Does it matter which AI agent we use?

No. We work on the content your agent answers from, and we test the agent the way your customers use it.

Who will we work with?

One senior knowledge lead runs your account from the audit through each monthly review, and all work is reviewed before it reaches you.

How is pricing set?

Plans start at $7,500 a month for a single product help center. Larger knowledge bases and multiple products are quoted after the free assessment.

What does the contract look like?

A master services agreement and a statement of work, with your NDA or ours. Three months to start, then month to month.

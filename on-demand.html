The accuracy guarantee

At the end of the Diagnostic we agree, in writing, on a target score for your test set. If the 90-Day Program has not reached it by day 90, we keep working at no charge until it does.

It applies when you publish approved changes within five business days and keep the same AI agent vendor during the program.

Platforms we work in

Salesforce Knowledge and Agentforce, Confluence, SharePoint, Adobe Experience Manager Guides, Heretto, Git and Markdown docs, and DITA XML. If your help center runs on something else, the test and the content work still apply. We will tell you on the first call what we have and have not used.

What we need from you

  • Editor access to your help center or knowledge base
  • An export of recent support tickets, for the test set
  • One subject matter expert for about two hours a week to approve changes

Contracts

We sign your NDA or use ours. Work runs under a master services agreement with a statement of work for each engagement. Invoices come from AJD Holdings LLC, doing business as Doc & Writer.

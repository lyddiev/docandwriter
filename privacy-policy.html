Privacy policy

Effective October 2026.

This policy covers docandwriter.com, run by AJD Holdings LLC, doing business as Doc & Writer (“we”).

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you use a form on this site or email us, we receive your name, email address, company, and anything you write, such as your help center address.
  • Server logs. Our host, Netlify, records standard request data such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, to run and secure the site.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts on this site.

How we use it

We use what you send to reply to you, to prepare a report or proposal you asked for, and to deliver work under a signed agreement. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Service providers

  • Netlify hosts this site and receives form submissions.
  • Calendly handles call bookings if you choose to book a call. Its own privacy policy applies to what you enter there.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we exchange.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for up to two years, and client records for as long as our contracts and tax rules require.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing support@docandwriter.com. We reply within 30 days.

Client data

Data we receive during an engagement, such as ticket exports, is covered by our agreement and NDA with that client, not by this policy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

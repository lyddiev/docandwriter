Work and results

Doc & Writer client work, and the contract engagements where Alexander built the methods we use.

Meta, global support operations

Alexander’s contract engagement, 2025 to 2026. Eight portfolios, 14,000+ agents, 15+ markets.

  • Lifted case accuracy from 88% to 96% across 15+ markets by putting QA protocols and governance on the 100+ knowledge base articles agents worked from.
  • Ran the knowledge base audit and overhaul that took an internal AI agent for advertiser support from experiment to production, where it lifted CSAT 3 to 5% in key workflows.
  • Built a 16-tag feedback triage system that routed content gaps reported by agents to the owning writer and tracked each one to closure.
  • Expanded machine translation of the knowledge base from 16 to 30 languages, so agents in every market worked from the same source content.
  • Cut average handling time from 19 minutes to 9 across 22 products by taking an AI payment verification tool from business case to deployment.

Meta, enterprise support

Alexander’s contract engagement, 2022 to 2023.

  • Eliminated 90% of client escalations within 60 days by overhauling issue triage and case handling.
  • Delivered an enterprise CMS migration for a technical publications library with zero downtime and full data integrity.
  • Restructured the help center content framework for enterprise support teams.

O-I Glass

Alexander’s engagements, 2019 to 2020 and 2023 to 2024. A $250M R&D program across three plants.

  • Migrated the DITA XML knowledge base to a new content platform with single sign-on for 10,000+ users, lifting adoption 5x.
  • Authored and governed 900+ standard operating procedures across three plants as the single source of truth for operations.
  • Shortened onboarding 35% for new engineers by rolling out Microsoft Copilot to 300 of them and standardizing onboarding.
  • Reduced project timelines from 25 days to 4 with a QA validation standard the organization adopted.

Hunter Douglas

Alexander’s contract engagement, 2020 to 2021.

  • Migrated product content to Adobe Experience Manager Guides across 12 product lines.
  • Scaled product, installation, and training content from 3 pilot sites to thousands of dealers while keeping BOM and SKU data in sync.

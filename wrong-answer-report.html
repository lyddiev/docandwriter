Free wrong-answer report

We ask your help center and AI agent 25 questions your customers ask, and show you which answers are wrong and which article caused each one.

We reply within one business day. If your help center or agent sits behind a login, we will ask for a test account. We use what you send only to prepare your report. Privacy policy.

What you get

  • 25 questions, each marked right or wrong
  • The article behind every wrong answer, and why it misled
  • A rough cost of those wrong answers, using public benchmarks
  • A 10-minute screen recording walking through the worst three

Delivered within five business days. No call required, and no obligation.
